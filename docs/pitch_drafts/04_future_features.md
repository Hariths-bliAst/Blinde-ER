# Slide 6: Future Ecosystem Roadmap (With Video Script)
> **Project:** Blind-ER — Context-Aware Book-to-Audiobook Scanner
> **Owner:** Nijad & Hariths

## Expanding Beyond Books: The Complete Blind-ER Ecosystem
1. **Audio Pitch & Spatial Navigation Training Games:**
   - Interactive, gamified auditory challenges that help newly blind and visually impaired users sharpen pitch discrimination, echolocation cues, and spatial audio mapping.
2. **IoT Smart Home Camera Integration:**
   - Connects Blind-ER to edge IoT home cameras to provide proactive, context-aware voice descriptions of home safety—identifying visitors at the door, checking if stove burners are left on, alerting to floor obstacles, and finding misplaced everyday objects via voice query.
3. **Multi-Character Voice Acting & Offline Edge Models:**
   - Distinct expressive TTS voices for different characters in dialogue, plus quantized on-device CRNN + compact LLM inference for 100% offline reading.
