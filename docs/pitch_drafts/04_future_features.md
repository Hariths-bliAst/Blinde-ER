## Beyond the MVP: The Complete Blind-ER Ecosystem
1. **Audio Pitch & Spatial Navigation Training Games:**
   - Gamified auditory exercises that train pitch recognition, echolocation cues, and spatial audio orientation for newly visually impaired users.
2. **IoT Smart Home Camera Integration:**
   - Connect Blind-ER to edge IoT home cameras to provide proactive, context-aware voice descriptions of room state, visitors, stove/appliance safety, and misplaced objects.
3. **Multi-Voice Character Narration & Offline Edge Inference:**
   - Distinct expressive voices for dialogue characters and quantized on-device CRNN + compact LLM inference for offline reading.
