## Core Problem
- Conventional OCR-to-Speech readers translate scanned book pages word-for-word with robotic prosody and broken formatting (headers, page numbers, hyphenated line-breaks read out loud).
- Visually impaired and partially blind users lose emotional nuance, literary tone, idiomatic meaning, and narrative flow when reading books or translated texts.
- Existing scanning apps rely on small visual buttons that are difficult to operate without sight.
